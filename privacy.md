# Clarity Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Clarity ("the app") is developed by Nihar Vishwakarma. Contact: YOUR_EMAIL_HERE

## Summary

Your data stays on your device. If you choose to turn on Google Drive backup, it is also stored in a private area of your own Google Drive. The developer runs no servers, cannot see your data, and does not sell or share it.

## Data the app handles

- **Tasks, calendar settings, appearance settings and widget settings.** Stored on your device.
- **Widget wallpaper images** you choose. Stored on your device.
- **Password vault entries.** Stored on your device in a file encrypted with a key derived from your master password. The master password is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, the vault cannot be recovered.
- **Biometric unlock (optional).** Handled by Android. The app does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect a Google account, the app asks for access to the Drive "app data" folder only (`drive.appdata`). This is a hidden folder that only Clarity can use. The app cannot see your other Drive files.

- The app stores your tasks and settings, your wallpaper images and your encrypted vault file there.
- Tasks and settings are stored as readable data in that folder. The vault file stays encrypted with your master password.
- The app reads your Google account email address to show which account is connected.
- This data goes directly between your device and Google. The developer never receives it.
- You can delete the backup from Settings > Backup & Restore in the app, and you can revoke access at any time in your Google Account under Security > Third-party access.

Google's handling of your data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Clarity's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** only for the optional Drive backup.
- **Notifications and alarms:** to show task reminders.
- **Biometrics:** to unlock the vault if you turn this on.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, analytics or tracking.
- No sale or sharing of data with third parties.
- No collection of your data by the developer.

## Retention and deletion

Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Drive backups remain until you delete them in the app or from your Google Account.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
